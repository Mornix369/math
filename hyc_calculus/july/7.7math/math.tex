\section*{Related Rates Practice}

\subsection*{3. \quad [20 points]}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.35\textwidth]{3.png}
\end{center}

A container has the shape of an open right circular cone, as shown in the figure above. The height of the container is $10\text{ cm}$ and the diameter of the opening is $10\text{ cm}$. Water in the container is evaporating so that its depth $h$ is changing at the constant rate of
\[
\frac{dh}{dt}=-\frac{3}{10}\text{ cm/hr}.
\]

\textit{Note:} The volume of a cone of height $h$ and radius $r$ is given by
\[
V=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2h.
\]

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Find the volume $V$ of water in the container when $h=5\text{ cm}$. Indicate units of measure.
    
    \item Find the rate of change of the volume of water in the container, with respect to time, when $h=5\text{ cm}$. Indicate units of measure.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{1. \quad [20 points]}

A water tank has the shape of an open right circular cone. The height of the tank is $12\text{ cm}$ and the diameter of the opening is $8\text{ cm}$. Water is being poured into the tank so that the water depth $h$ is increasing at the constant rate
\[
\frac{dh}{dt}=\frac{1}{4}\text{ cm/min}.
\]

\textit{Note:} The volume of a cone of height $h$ and radius $r$ is
\[
V=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2h.
\]

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Find the volume of water in the tank when $h=6\text{ cm}$.
    
    \item Find the rate of change of the volume of water in the tank when $h=6\text{ cm}$. Indicate units of measure.
\end{enumerate}
